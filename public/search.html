Degrees or hours?

Both work. RA in hours is multiplied by 15 to get degrees, so 08h55m = 133.75°. Write hours as 08h55m10.8s, 08 55 10.8 or 08:55:10.8, and Dec as −7.24, −7d14m42s or −07 14 42.

Field of view

How wide a square of sky to show. 10′ (a third of the Moon) is a good start. Fast stars move a long way, so they get 15′. Bigger fields take longer to prepare. 2′ is a zoom into a 5′ patch.
