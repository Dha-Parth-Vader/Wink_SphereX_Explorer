Welcome to SkyBlink, the astronomy blinker

Pick any spot in the sky and SkyBlink plays NASA's SPHEREx pictures of it, one date after another, like a little flip book. Stars that stay put stay still. Anything that moves or changes brightness jumps out at you.

That's how Clyde Tombaugh found Pluto in 1930: he flipped between two photos of the same patch of sky. Now you can do it with a space telescope that sees the whole sky in 102 colours of infrared light.

Our goals

✦

Amateur accessibility

No login and no jargon. Type a position or tap a famous target, press Play, and look. Everything else is explained along the way.

🔭

Real NASA data for any spot in the sky

Every picture comes from SPHEREx through NASA's infrared archive at IRSA. 100 targets open instantly, and our live server fetches anywhere else.

🌐

See in 102 colours

SPHEREx splits infrared light into 102 bands across 6 detectors. Switch detectors, sweep the bands in the Sky Dome, and spot cold brown dwarfs that only glow at the longest wavelengths.
